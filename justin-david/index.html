Never done one? Here is the whole idea

All-Inclusive, Explained

You pay once, before you go. Then for the whole week you never see a bill. You walk up to the bar, order a drink, and walk away. You sit down at a restaurant, order off the menu, and leave. That is it. No signing, no tabs, no doing the math at dinner.

✔

What is included

  • Your suite for all five nights
  • Every meal — breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks in between
  • Drinks, including cocktails, wine, beer and the swim-up bars
  • Your minibar, restocked
  • Room service
  • Pools, beach, loungers, activities and nightly entertainment
$

What costs extra

  • Spa treatments
  • Excursions off the resort
  • A few special dinners or top-shelf bottles at some resorts
  • Airport transfers — I add these once you choose

Anything extra gets charged to your room, so you will know before you sign for it.

🍽

How the restaurants work

These are not buffet resorts. Each one has several sit-down restaurants — Italian, French, Asian, Mexican, seafood — where you order from a menu like you would at home. There is usually a buffet and a 24-hour café too, for when you just want something easy.

Most have a dress code at dinner: think long pants and a nice shirt. Pack a couple of outfits for it.

★

What “Preferred Club” means

Three of these quotes are for the Preferred Club at Secrets — their upgraded level. You get better room locations, a private lounge with breakfast, snacks and premium drinks, your own check-in, and a concierge to book dinners and spa time for you.

It is the “someone else handles it” level, and for a first all-inclusive it is a really nice way to do it.

The other coast

Puerto Vallarta

Before the Caribbean options, one on the Pacific side — and it is the least expensive of everything here. Puerto Vallarta is a proper town rather than a hotel strip: cobbled streets, a seafront malecón, galleries and restaurants you walk to. It has also been one of the most openly gay-friendly places in Mexico for decades, and the Zona Romántica is the heart of it.

Akumal · Riviera Maya

Secrets Akumal

The turtle beach.

Akumal means “place of the turtle” in Mayan, and it earns the name — the bay is calm and clear, and the sea turtles live right there. You can snorkel with them. It is the quietest, most relaxed setting on this page.

Three pools, each with its own mood, plus a big spa with couples cabins. Restaurants include Portofino (Italian), Bordeaux (French), Himitsu (Asian), Oceana (seafood) and El Patio (Mexican).

The one near the fun.

Sleek, modern and grown-up, on the beach in Playa del Carmen — about ten minutes from Fifth Avenue if you want a night of shops, bars and people-watching in town.

Atelier Playa Mujeres

For the foodies with taste.

Built as a showcase of Mexican art and design — there is literally an art walk through the resort. It sits between the sea and a golf course, just north of Cancun, and it is the closest to the airport of the five.

Thirteen restaurants and twelve bars, including Maria Dolores (contemporary Mexican), Cilento (Italian), Takeshi (Asian) and Cala di Luna (Mediterranean). Rooftop pool, 24-hour room service, and tips are included.
