The resort

Renovated under Hilton, returned to Dreams

The rare DR resort where the buildings are recent and the wedding venues are newer still.

Dreams La Romana sits on a secluded white-sand stretch of Playa Bayahibe on the southern coast, framed by tropical forest with a coral reef just offshore. It is Unlimited-Luxury®, built for families and couples together — a sprawling water park with tube slides and a lazy river, Kids and Teens Clubs, a casino, a full spa with hydrotherapy, and live nightly entertainment.

Its history matters. The resort left the Dreams brand in November 2018 to become Hilton La Romana, was fully renovated across 2019, and split into two separate hotels. It returned to Dreams in June 2025 when Hyatt completed its acquisition of Playa. The adults-only half is now Secrets La Romana next door.

So the hardware dates from 2019 rather than the 1990s, and the wedding spaces are newer again — the Las Palmas ballroom and Foyer assets are dated early 2026. Any source quoting 756 rooms is describing the pre-split property and is wrong.

For weddings it is unusually versatile: ten venues running from a 35-guest seaside garden to an 11,000 sq ft ballroom seating 930, plus a private pier for ceremonies over the water.

Planning notes

  • Ceremony types: civil/legal, symbolic, vow renewal, Hindu, same-sex symbolic
  • Wedding specialists here are certified by the South Asian Wedding Institute
  • Music to 11pm outdoors, midnight in the ballroom, and 10pm on the Pier
  • Sunset Garden West carries a venue fee on top of the package
  • Big Day Preview costs $500 and converts to a $500 credit when the wedding is booked
  • 25 minutes from La Romana (LRM), roughly 45 minutes from Punta Cana (PUJ)

Ceremony and reception numbers diverge sharply here. The Private Beach Gazebo seats 100 for a ceremony but only 35 for dinner; the Pier is 50 and 12. Always check the reception figure — it is the binding one.

Two things to confirm with the resort: the pool count and the weddings-per-day cap are not published anywhere, and El Camino's marketing photograph is a rendering, so check it has actually opened before selling it.
