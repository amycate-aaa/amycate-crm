The resort

Big, secluded, and built for a mixed-age group

AAA Four Diamond, Unlimited-Luxury®, and far enough up the coast to feel genuinely private.

Dreams Onyx sits on a remote, palm-studded stretch of Uvero Alto beach on the north-east coast, about forty-five minutes from Punta Cana International — far enough from the Bávaro strip to feel private, close enough for an easy transfer.

It has 806 suites, and every single one has a private furnished balcony or terrace with its own hot tub, which is unusual at this price. The resort splits into three moods: a 304-suite family wing around the Jungle Waterpark with four slides and a lazy river; a Preferred Club with its own lounge, restaurant, bars and two pools; and an adults-only Preferred Club section with plunge-pool suites and an oceanfront master.

Six pools, a spa with a hydrotherapy circuit, a casino, a theatre and two dozen restaurants and bars keep a mixed group busy. Adults 18+ also get full exchange privileges next door at Breathless Punta Cana, which effectively doubles the dining and nightlife.

This was Now Onyx Punta Cana before the rebrand — older reviews may still use that name.

Club level

Preferred Club

Two club pools — one for families, one adults-only — plus a lounge, a club-only breakfast restaurant and three private bars.

Unusually complete for a Dreams property: the club here has its own restaurant for breakfast and three of its own bars, including a swim-up.

  • Personalised check-in and check-out with concierge
  • Daily continental breakfast in the lounge
  • Afternoon hot and cold hors d'oeuvres
  • Exquisite desserts and premium liquors
  • Two Preferred Club pools — one family, one adults-only
  • Pool concierge and bar service
  • Preferred Club restaurant for breakfast
  • Club-only bars including a swim-up bar
  • Suites in prime locations
  • Upgraded minibar, bath amenities and room-service menu

Worth checking at booking: the adults-only boundary is not absolute. Not every Preferred Club Ocean View and Master Suite Ocean Front sits inside the adults-only section, so if a couple is buying that atmosphere it needs to be requested and confirmed rather than assumed.

Planning notes

  • Ceremony types: Catholic, civil/legal, Hindu, Sikh, symbolic, renewal, same-sex civil and symbolic
  • Wedding in Paradise is complimentary with 7 nights in a Preferred Club Junior Suite Partial Ocean View or higher, or 5 rooms in any category for 3 nights
  • Beyond Memorable needs 75 cumulative paid room nights and booking a year ahead
  • Music to 11pm on Preferred Beach and midnight in the Ballroom; 10pm everywhere else
  • Stone Beach and the Preferred Adults Pool carry a venue fee
  • All weddings must be booked at least 30 days out; officiant fees are extra

The indoor rain plan is small. The Ballroom holds only 70, so a large wedding has no full-size indoor fallback. Worth raising for hurricane-season dates.
