The two-bedroom options

Riu Cancún and Riu Palace Las Americas, side by side

Both are connecting rooms: two complete rooms joined by a short private hallway with its own outside door. You book the pair as one unit and the hallway door locks, so it's private to your party — two bathrooms, two balconies, two TVs. There isn't a door between the bedrooms themselves; the hallway is the link.

Riu Cancún — Two Bedroom Double Room, Ocean View

Two ocean-view double rooms as one unit · sleeps up to 6

Space
About 646 sq ft in total — two rooms of roughly 323–377 sq ft (30–35 m²).
Beds
Each room: one king (200×200 cm) or two small doubles (125×200 cm). Two kings, four doubles, or one of each — say which when booking.
Sleeps
Up to 6 (3 per room).
View
Every room at Riu Cancún faces the sea; each of the two has its own ocean-view balcony or terrace.
In each room
Sitting area with sofa, satellite TV, air conditioning and ceiling fan, minibar restocked daily, liquor dispenser with four spirits, coffee maker, safe, iron, double-sink bathroom with shower and hairdryer, free Wi-Fi.
Good to know
Priced as two rooms. The junior suite and suite categories don't come as connecting pairs — the two-bedroom is built from the ocean-view double.

Riu Palace Las Americas — Connecting Junior Suite (2 rooms)

Two junior suites as one unit · sleeps up to 6 · garden or ocean view

Space
About 754 sq ft in total — two junior suites of roughly 377 sq ft (35 m²) each, with a split-level lounge in each.
Beds
In the connecting configuration each room has two double beds (125×200 cm). Single junior suites offer a king; the connecting pairs are set up as two doubles — confirm at booking if a king matters.
Sleeps
Up to 6 (3 per room).
View
Garden or resort view as standard; an Ocean View connecting version is also sold, each room with its own sea-view balcony or terrace.
In each room
Sunken lounge with sofa, satellite flat-screen TV, air conditioning and ceiling fan, minibar, liquor dispenser with four spirits, coffee maker, safe, iron and board, free Wi-Fi, renovated bathroom with double sinks and walk-in shower (no tub).
Palace extras
24-hour room service included, premium spirits, the 24-hour Lounge for late food, Capuchino patisserie, free use of Riu Cancún next door including Pacha.
Good to know
Not a suite with two bedrooms — two genuinely separate junior suites behind one locked hallway door, which for two couples is usually the better arrangement.
