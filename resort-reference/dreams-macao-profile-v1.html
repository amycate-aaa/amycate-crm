The resort

A new-build on a UNESCO beach, with a second resort arriving next door

Modern, compact and genuinely family-friendly — the strongest waterpark property in Punta Cana for a wedding with children in the group.

Dreams Macao Beach is the first and only all-inclusive resort on Macao Beach, on the northeastern coast of the Dominican Republic. It is a modern build rather than a refurbished older property, and it shows: 500 rooms and suites in calm neutrals with teal accents, connected infinity pools, a full waterpark with a lazy river, and the Spa by Pevonia®.

Where it wins is families. Every standard category is a 603 sq ft Junior Suite sleeping four, the waterpark and Explorer’s Club are included, and the layout is walkable rather than sprawling. For a wedding party travelling with children, that combination is hard to beat on this coast.

What to raise on the call. Macao Beach is beautiful and gets noticeably less seaweed than the Bavaro strip, but the surf is real — waves run strong from December through March and it is not a beach for toddlers to swim off. The pools carry that load. The transfer is also about 45 minutes from Punta Cana International, against roughly 20 for the Bavaro resorts.

The 2026 change. Hyatt is opening the adults-only Secrets Macao Beach Punta Cana — 406 rooms — directly adjacent, with free-flow access between the two resorts for guests of either. For a wedding party split between couples and families, that is a real advantage. It also means construction next door until it opens.
