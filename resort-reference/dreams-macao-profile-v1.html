Overview

Dreams Macao Beach Punta Cana

The only all-inclusive on Macao Beach — wide, protected sand, and room for everyone you love.

Uncover serenity, adventure, and romance at the first and only all-inclusive, family-friendly resort on Macao Beach. Located on the northeastern coast of the Dominican Republic, Dreams Macao Beach Punta Cana provides the perfect backdrop for your romantic wedding celebration in paradise. Enjoy direct access to a pristine white-sand beach and the crystal-clear waters of the Caribbean Sea. With four shimmering pools, elegant rooms and suites, limitless gourmet dining at 10 restaurants, endless activities including a full waterpark, and the world-class Spa by Pevonia®, every moment of your stay will be unforgettable.
