The resort

Hilton Vallarta Riviera All-Inclusive Resort

A 444-room all-inclusive on a private stretch of Banderas Bay, five minutes south of Puerto Vallarta's Zona Hotelera and about 34 minutes from the airport. Every room is oceanfront with a private balcony, and the whole resort faces the water in a single curved tower rising to the fourteenth floor. Seven restaurants and six bars and cafes are included, alongside two oceanfront infinity pools - one family, one adults-only with a swim-up bar - a private beach, a kids club for ages four to twelve, a teen zone, a 24-hour fitness centre and nightly live entertainment. Higher floors hold the adults-only section and the Enclave club tier, with its own thirteenth-floor lounge. For weddings and groups there is a 14,863 sq ft oceanfront Beach Club, the Gran Siqueiros ballroom that divides into four salons, a rooftop Sky Bar terrace and eight small breakout rooms.
