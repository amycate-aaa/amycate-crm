The resort

Every room faces the water

Hilton Vallarta Riviera sits on a private stretch of Banderas Bay in the Zona Hotelera Sur, about thirty-four minutes from Puerto Vallarta airport and under fifteen from the old town. It is one curved tower, fourteen floors, turned to face the Pacific — and that is the single fact that shapes the whole stay. All 444 rooms are oceanfront, every one of them with a private balcony. There is no garden-view category to avoid and no bad side of the building.

The layout stacks upward. The lower floors hold the standard rooms; the higher floors hold the premier views and, on twelve to fourteen, the Enclave suites with jetted tubs on the balcony. The adults-only section runs vertically through the tower as its own set of room categories, with the Sky Bar, the Martini Lounge and the adults-only pool attached to it. So a family and a couple travelling together can be in the same building, on the same rate, with genuinely different days.

Seven restaurants and six bars are included, along with two oceanfront infinity pools, a private beach, a kids club for four to twelve, a teen zone, a twenty-four-hour fitness centre and nightly live entertainment. For a wedding or a group there is a 14,863 sq ft oceanfront Beach Club, the Gran Siqueiros ballroom that divides into four salons, a rooftop Sky Bar terrace and eight small breakout rooms.

It suits a mixed-age wedding party, a family that wants a real kids club, and couples who want an adults-only feel without booking an adults-only resort. It is a large, busy, family-forward property — not a hideaway.

Club level

Enclave

Enclave is the club tier, and its centre is a private lounge on the thirteenth floor with the bay in front of it. Open daily from 6 am to 11 pm: breakfast from 7 to 11, premium drinks from 6 to 8 in the evening, and coffee and dry snacks all day. There is a concierge desk, premium WiFi, a computer and printing station, a library space and screens.

Enclave rooms are the ones with jetted tubs — on the balcony in the two entry Enclave categories, on the terrace in the Governor and Presidential suites. Six categories carry it: the Enclave Ocean View king and two-queen rooms, the Ocean Front Master Suite, the Governor Suite, the Presidential Suite and the two-bedroom suite. The Vallarta Suite also carries club access.

Dining & drinks

Seven restaurants, six bars, all included

Everything below is in the rate, including twenty-four-hour room service and the minibar, which is restocked daily. Dinner reservations are made through the resort’s guest app. The dress code at the three specialty restaurants — La Luce, Maxal and Sunan — is casual with no beachwear; everywhere else beachwear is fine as long as you are wearing shoes, a top and bottoms, and nobody goes in wet.

Pools, beach & water

Two infinity pools on the sand

The pools. Two oceanfront infinity pools, both looking straight down the bay. One is the family pool and the centre of the daytime programme — aqua fitness, aqua strong, pool games, pool casino. The other is adults-only, with the Pool Bar serving from the water and to your lounger, from 10 am to 7 pm.

Bali beds. Cabana-style Bali beds can be reserved on the beach or by the pool, each one a private spot for the day. They are an extra, and you can add sparkling wine, gourmet bites or a spa treatment to one.

The Sky Bar. The rooftop is adults-only and has two whirlpools and cabanas alongside the bar. It runs from 1 pm until 1 am and is the best place on the property for the sunset.

The beach. A private stretch of Pacific coastline on Banderas Bay, with palms, cabanas, beach volleyball and beach soccer, and the Beach Club at the end of it. Puerto Vallarta is a Pacific-facing bay rather than the Caribbean, so the water has more movement to it than clients coming from Cancún will expect — worth saying out loud before anyone books.

There is no water park or splash park at this resort, and no separate children’s pool. If a splash park is what a family is after, I will point you somewhere else.

Weddings & groups

Ceremonies here are symbolic or non-denominational, led by a bilingual officiant and a justice of the peace; a legal ceremony is possible with extra requirements and fees. The wedding team is Equality Institute-certified and the resort actively welcomes LGBTQ+ weddings.

Days and evenings

What there is to do

Included by day. Aqua strong, aqua fitness, cardio dance, yoga, beach volleyball, beach soccer, bocce ball, pool games and pool casino. Cultural workshops run through the week, including jewellery-making and traditional crafts. The fitness centre is open twenty-four hours, with group classes and certified instructors leading sessions on the beach.

Included by night. Karaoke at the Mojito Lounge, a silent disco, a Vegas-style casino night with poker, blackjack and roulette, a live DJ at the Martini Lounge for adults, and live music in the lobby that changes nightly — sax, trumpet, cello, violin, a duo. The set shows are the Catrinas Show, the Latino Show and an aqua light show with acrobatic dancers.

Kids and teens. The Kids Club takes ages four to twelve, in a bright indoor space with arts and crafts, a lounge with bean bags and a HDTV, and a Baby Zone soft-play area for babies and toddlers. The Teen Zone runs street art, photography, contemporary dance and music workshops by day, beach games, volleyball, unmotorised water sports and surfing outside, and movie and pizza nights, breakdance classes, themed parties, talent contests and a DJ nightclub after dark. Unlimited ice cream throughout.

eforea Spa. Massages, facials and body treatments, plus a hydrotherapy circuit — sauna, steam room, sensation shower and plunge pools. Open 9 am to 7 pm daily. There are facials designed for children as well as adults. The spa is the one part of the resort that sits outside the all-inclusive plan; treatments are priced per person in Mexican pesos and include VAT, and the hydrotherapy circuit comes as a courtesy with any other service. Book at least twenty-four hours ahead.

Beyond the resort. Downtown Puerto Vallarta and the Zona Romántica, the Malécon boardwalk, Marina Vallarta and its golf course, Los Arcos de Mismaloya for snorkelling, and Isla Cuale. Excursions carry an extra charge and are arranged through the concierge.

Worth knowing before you book

Check-in is 3 pm and check-out is 12 pm; late check-out is $20 per person per hour. The minimum age to check in is eighteen. Parking is complimentary and WiFi is free in the room and the lobby. The resort is entirely smoke-free, indoors and out, and pets are not permitted — service animals are.

Airport transfers are not part of the all-inclusive plan. The concierge arranges them with a third-party supplier for an additional fee — send flight details at least forty-eight hours before arrival — or I will build them into your group rate, which is usually the better answer.
