Overview

Dreams Bahia Mita Surf & Spa Resort

Discover Dreams Bahia Mita with stylish suites, a family-favorite waterpark, oceanfront infinity pool and surf-inspired activities.

Dreams Bahia Mita Surf & Spa Resort marries golden sand beaches and mountaintop views with luxurious suites and daily activities for a family-friendly, all-inclusive vacation. Designed by architect Sordo Madaleno, this eco-friendly resort sits on Banderas Bay, about 50 minutes from Puerto Vallarta and 35 minutes from Nuevo Vallarta. All 421 suites offer tropical or ocean views, modern décor, and refined amenities like a daily refreshed mini-bar and 24-hour room service. The Unlimited-Luxury® program means everything is included — no wristbands or gratuities required — with activities from kayaking and surf lessons to golf, plus a Marietas Island-inspired Secrets Spa, eight dining venues and six bars. Guests also have access to the adjoining adults-only Secrets Bahia Mita, which adds flexible wedding venue options.
