Overview

Secrets Royal Beach Punta Cana

Adults-only Unlimited-Luxury® on 700 yards of Bávaro beach — eight wedding locations, from barefoot sand ceremonies to a ballroom for 200.

Secrets Royal Beach Punta Cana is an adults-only Unlimited-Luxury® resort occupying a stunning position on one of Punta Cana's most coveted stretches of Caribbean coastline, where the natural beauty of the Dominican Republic's eastern shore provides a breathtaking backdrop for weddings and honeymoons. Five elegantly designed wedding venues span the resort's magnificent beachfront grounds, offering settings that range from romantic barefoot ceremonies on the white sand to sophisticated open-air receptions overlooking the turquoise sea. The resort's culinary excellence, premium beverage service, and world-class spa ensure that every guest experiences the highest standard of Unlimited-Luxury® hospitality throughout the celebration. With Punta Cana International Airport just 20 minutes away and a destination that has become synonymous with Caribbean paradise, Secrets Royal Beach Punta Cana makes it effortless to gather friends and family from around the world for the wedding of a lifetime.

Resort Notes

Oceana serves breakfast and lunch to Preferred Club guests only; dinner is open to all guests. Reservations are never required except for the Teppanyaki tables at Himitsu. Casual Elegance applies at dinner in the a la carte restaurants and at La Riviera. Two of the four pools, the lazy river and the Preferred Club lounge and bar are exclusive to Preferred Club guests. Four outdoor jetted pools are available in addition to the four swimming pools. The casino is open to adult guests of Secrets Royal Beach and Dreams Royal Beach, and additional charges apply. Small dogs are welcome in a required room category for a fee.
