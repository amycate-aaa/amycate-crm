The Resort

A beach on one side, a lagoon on the other

Hilton's flagship all-inclusive in Cancún — “Experience the extraordinary. All included.”

Hilton Cancun Mar Caribe sits on a slim stretch of the Hotel Zone with the Caribbean on one flank and Nichupté Lagoon on the other, about twenty minutes from Cancún International. Five hundred and forty rooms and suites step down toward a wide, calm swimming beach, and almost every one of them looks at water.

The whole property is organised around water: thirteen pools of different temperaments, from the quiet adults-only decks to the four rooftop pools at Chala Sky Bar, plus a splash pool for small children. Everything is included — twelve restaurants and bars, unlimited drinks, a minibar restocked daily, 24-hour room service, day and night entertainment, wellness classes, kids' and teens' clubs, a 24-hour fitness centre and a full spa with a hydrotherapy circuit set in the gardens.

For a wedding, the shape of it matters: the resort hosts one wedding per day, so the couple is never sharing the beach with another party. Five distinct ceremony and reception spaces run from a 200-guest beach setup to a 30-seat private garden, with an indoor ballroom on the fourteenth floor as the weather plan.

Club Level

The Enclave

The resort's club tier — beachfront rooms, a private lounge, and a rooftop reserved for club guests by day.

Enclave is not a floor, it's a wing: every Enclave category sits on the beachfront side of the property, from a 441 sq ft two-queen room to a two-bedroom suite with its own plunge pool. Ten categories in all.

  • Private check-in and check-out
  • Enclave Lounge access, 7am–11pm
  • Daily breakfast served in the lounge
  • Premium spirits and cocktails
  • Chef-crafted tapas through the day
  • Dedicated Enclave concierge desk
  • Chala Sky Bar — exclusive to Enclave guests by day, four rooftop pools
  • Reserved beachfront section with extra space
  • In-room welcome amenity on arrival
  • Upgraded minibar
  • Nightly turndown service
  • Souvenir beach bag
  • One complimentary spa hydrotherapy session for two per stay

Why it matters for a wedding group: Chala Sky Bar is both the Enclave daytime perk and one of the reception venues. Putting the couple and immediate family in Enclave gives them the rooftop, the reserved beach and the lounge as a private gathering space in the days around the wedding.

Dining & Drinks

Twelve venues, all included

No reservation fees, no à-la-carte charges, and 24-hour room service throughout.

Restaurants

Vela

International buffet · Breakfast 7–11am, lunch 12:30–5pm

A family-friendly buffet with a wide international spread, plus a rodizio-style carvery. Where most of the group will start the day.

Maxal

Mexican · Dinner 6–11pm

Authentic Mexican cooking built on vibrant regional flavours and locally sourced ingredients, with an outdoor terrace.

La Luce

Italian & Mediterranean · Dinner 6–11pm

A ristorante-style room and terrace overlooking Nichupté Lagoon — the prettiest sunset table on property and a natural rehearsal-dinner option.

Noriku

Asian & teppanyaki · Dinner 6–11pm

Pan-Asian cooking with live teppanyaki tables. Book the teppanyaki side early for groups — it's the one venue that fills first.

Steakhouse

Steak & grill · Dinner 6–11pm

A curated cut list paired with the mixologists' cocktail menu. The most formal room on property.

Seasalt

Poolside grill & bar · 10am–6pm

Poolside restaurant and bar with pizzas, snacks and drinks all afternoon. Casual, swimsuits welcome.

Azulinda Café

Café & sweets · Open 24 hours

All-day coffee shop for sandwiches and sweet treats — genuinely open around the clock, which matters on late-arrival nights.

Room Service

In-room dining · 24 hours

Full all-inclusive dining delivered to the room or suite, at no extra charge.
