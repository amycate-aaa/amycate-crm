The resort

A family resort sharing its grounds with an adults-only sister

Strong for mixed-age groups: water park and teens club on one side, nightclub, casino and spa a short walk away at Secrets.

486 rooms and suites on Bávaro Beach, 23 minutes from PUJ. All-ages, AAA Four Diamond, and part of World of Hyatt. The Preferred Club section functions as a resort-within-a-resort with its own motor lobby, lounge, pools, restaurants and hydromassage area.

The thing to hold in your head when selling this property: the spa, the nightclub, the casino and Oceana restaurant are all on the Secrets side. Dreams has its own theatre at the Main Plaza, its own pools and its own dining. Guests move freely between the two, but a client expecting a spa on their own side will be surprised.

No resort shuttle — guests arrange their own transfers unless booked as a group. Group discounts and VIP benefits start at 10 rooms, with a dedicated groups coordinator.

Pools, beach & nightlife

Five pools, a water park and a nightclub next door

Pools. Main pool with swim-up bar and reserved Preferred Club section · Preferred Club pool with swim-up bar · pool by the plaza · kids' pool · Preferred Club kids' pool · hot tub. No adults-only pool — the split is family vs Preferred Club.

Water park. Seven slides plus a splash park, 10am–6pm, height minimums on some slides. That's A Wrap is inside it.

Beach. Bávaro — palm-studded white sand. Beach volleyball, two beachfront bars, Seaside Grill serving on the sand. The resort publishes nothing about water entry, currents or sargassum. A third-party wedding site describes it as calm and swimmable, but that is not the resort's own claim.

Nightlife. On property: theatre and nightly shows at the Main Plaza, beach parties, theme nights, Halftime Sports Bar (18+), Core Zone teens bar. At Secrets: Eclipse Night Club (18+, 10:30pm–1:30am) and the casino (7pm–2am, slots from 3pm, adult-only, additional cost).

Pool count conflict. Activities page says six pools; homepage FAQ says five and itemises five. Both are resort copy.

Casino location conflict. The activities page places the casino at Secrets. The official resort map lists it as #78 under the Dreams legend. Unresolved.

Activities & spa

What fills the days

Land. Beach volleyball, tennis courts and clinics*, pickleball, basketball, discounted green fees at Punta Cana courses*.

Water. Kayaks, snorkelling, non-motorised water sports, intro scuba in the pool. Fishing and full scuba extra*.

Kids & teens. Explorer's Club ages 3–12, 9am–10pm, includes a beach camping night; children must be fully potty-trained. Core Zone ages 13–17, 2pm–12am. Babysitting $15/hr one child, $25/hr two, via concierge.

Fitness. Treadmills, ellipticals, free weights, yoga, dance, aqua aerobics. Full access 18+; 16–17 with an adult.

Spa — at Secrets, not on this property. 17,000+ sq ft. Hydrotherapy circuit, sauna, steam room, salon, outdoor yoga area, hydro-tub and waterfall pool. Whether the hydrotherapy circuit is included or charged is not stated anywhere — destinationweddings marks "Spa ($)". Note both Beyond Memorable and Elope in Luxury include comp hydrotherapy access, which implies it is normally chargeable.

Excursions. Nothing published. Booked through the concierge and the Hyatt Experiences portal; Amstar is the DMC.

*Additional cost.
