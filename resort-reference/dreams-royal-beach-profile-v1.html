The resort

Big enough to give everyone their own kind of day

486 rooms on Bávaro Beach, sharing its grounds with adults-only Secrets Royal Beach next door.

Some of your people want a swim-up bar by eleven in the morning. Some want the quiet end of the beach. Someone is bringing children, someone else wants a proper night out. Dreams Royal Beach is one of the few properties in Punta Cana that does all of those without anyone compromising.

It is all-ages and all-inclusive, with a water park, a teens club, an adults-only French restaurant, and a nightclub and casino a short walk away at the Secrets side of the complex.

Pools, beach & water

Five pools, a water park and Bávaro Beach

Pools. A main pool with a swim-up bar and a reserved Preferred Club section; a Preferred Club pool with its own swim-up bar; a pool by the plaza; a kids' pool; a Preferred Club kids' pool; and a hot tub. The split here is family versus Preferred Club rather than adults versus children — there is no adults-only pool.

The water park runs 10am to 6pm with seven slides and a splash park for younger children. That's A Wrap sits inside it, so lunch doesn't mean leaving.

The beach is Bávaro — palm-lined white sand, and one of the better stretches on the island. Beach volleyball, two beachfront bars, and Seaside Grill serving on the sand.

Booking as a group

Group discounts and VIP benefits start at ten rooms, with a dedicated groups coordinator. What that buys you beyond the rate: your price is locked against increases between now and the trip, roundtrip airport transfers are included for every guest, and your guests book through their own page at the locked rate so you are not chasing anybody.

It takes $100 a room to hold the block. The balance falls due 90 to 130 days before travel, and a group credit scales with the number of rooms.

Kids & teens

Explorer's Club runs 9am to 10pm for ages 3 to 12, including a beach camping night. Core Zone covers 13 to 17, 2pm to midnight, with its own non-alcoholic bar. Both are included.

Worth knowing for a mixed-age group: the Eclipse Night Club, the casino and the Halftime sports bar are all 18+, and the first two are on the Secrets side of the complex. For anyone younger the evening is the theatre, the shows, the beach parties and Core Zone.

Days

What there is to do

On the sand and the courts. Beach volleyball, tennis courts and clinics, pickleball, basketball, and discounted green fees at Punta Cana golf courses.

In the water. Kayaks, snorkelling, non-motorised water sports, and an introductory scuba lesson in the pool.

The spa. 17,000 sq ft next door at Secrets — hydrotherapy circuit, sauna, steam room, salon, outdoor yoga area and a waterfall pool.

Fitness. Treadmills, ellipticals and free weights, plus yoga, dance and aqua aerobics. Full access 18+, or 16–17 with an adult.

Off property. Full and half-day excursions across the island, booked through the concierge.
