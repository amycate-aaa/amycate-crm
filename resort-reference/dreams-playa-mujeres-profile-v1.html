Overview

Dreams Playa Mujeres Golf & Spa Resort

Beachfront Luxury in Mexico

Perfectly situated in the exclusive gated community of Playa Mujeres, within a world-famous golf course, rests the AAA Four Diamond Dreams Playa Mujeres Golf & Spa Resort. This all-suite resort boasts 502 luxuriously equipped suites surrounded by secluded white sand beaches and the turquoise Caribbean Sea. Three levels of swimming pools spanning over 65,000 square feet — including two infinity pools with ocean views, six whirlpools, a children's pool, a water park for little ones, a water park for all ages, and a lazy river. Located just 10 minutes from downtown Cancun and 35 minutes from Cancun International Airport. Guests enjoy Unlimited-Luxury® — everything included, no wristbands or gratuities required, with unlimited premium drinks at 13 bars and lounges (including 4 non-alcoholic bars for teens and tweens), gourmet à la carte dining, 24-hour room service, daily refreshed mini-bar, and pool and beach wait service. Guests 18+ also get walking access to the neighboring adults-only Secrets Playa Mujeres restaurants, bars and facilities (excluding its Preferred Club areas).

Resort Notes

The lazy river is a Preferred Club benefit and is not open to guests in standard room categories. L'Étoile is for ages 13 and over, and Spin becomes adults-only after dark. Guests aged 18 and over have complimentary evening access to the restaurants and bars at the adjoining adults-only Secrets Playa Mujeres through the Sip, Savor & See programme; Secrets' own Preferred Club areas are not included. The Greg Norman-designed Playa Mujeres Golf Club is about two minutes from the lobby, with discounted green fees for resort guests — golf is not included in the rate. Explorer's Club takes children aged 3–12; Core Zone is for ages 13–17.

Excursions

  • Delphinus Playa Mujeres dolphin swim, on the resort grounds
  • Tours and excursions arranged through the resort, at additional cost
