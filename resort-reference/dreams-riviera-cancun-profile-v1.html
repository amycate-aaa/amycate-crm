Overview

Dreams Riviera Cancun Resort & Spa

Unlimited-Luxury® · All Ages · 486 Octagonal Suites · Riviera Maya

Tucked between lush tropical gardens and the white sands of the Riviera Maya — just steps from the town of Puerto Morelos and 25 minutes from Cancun International Airport — Dreams Riviera Cancun Resort & Spa is an AAA Four Diamond, Unlimited-Luxury® all-inclusive resort designed for families, couples, and groups. Its 486 signature octagonal-shaped suites are unlike anything else in the area: 70% face the ocean, and many feature private plunge pools. Kids thrive in the Mayan-themed Explorer's Club (pyramid climbing wall, planetarium, toddler playroom), while adults retreat to a world-class spa, an intimate wine cellar, and an adults-only infinity pool at sunset. Three pools total, a splash park with mini waterslides, 9 dining venues, 6 bars, nightly shows, beach volleyball, cooking classes, and a tequila tasting room make this resort a complete destination — whether you're celebrating a wedding or simply dreaming of the Caribbean.

    Resort Notes

    The infinity pool is adults-only. Explorer’s Club is for ages 3-12 and children must be fully potty-trained. The arcade is open to guests age 10 and older. Reservations are not needed for most restaurants; Teppanyaki seating at Himitsu is arranged at the concierge desk and fills early. Drones are not permitted on resort grounds.
