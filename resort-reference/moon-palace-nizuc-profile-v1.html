The resort

A family resort with a two-bedroom suite that actually sleeps seven

713 rooms inside the wider Moon Palace complex, with free access to the sister resort next door.

Nizuc sits inside the Moon Palace complex, which means your family has its own quieter resort to come back to and a much larger one to explore. Guests have full access to Moon Palace Cancún’s restaurants and bars as well as their own.

It is ten minutes from the airport — the shortest transfer of any resort in this comparison, which matters more than it sounds with small children and car seats.

Every room has a double whirlpool tub, a balcony, a premium liquor dispenser and a minibar restocked daily. Kids and teens seventeen and under stay free with one paying adult in the room.

Pools, beach & water

Winding pools, a water slide and a splash zone

Pools. Expansive winding pools, including a family-friendly section with a water slide, plus a splash zone for younger children and a handicap-accessible pool.

The FlowRider® double wave simulator runs 9am to 5pm at Moon Palace Cancún, a short walk or ride away, and is included. Private slots for up to eight can be arranged at extra cost.

The beach is white sand on the Caribbean. The marina closes every Monday for reef conservation.

Weddings & groups

Five wedding packages, from a complimentary ceremony up to Luxe.

Nizuc runs symbolic, civil and religious ceremonies with an on-site coordinator, and the wider Moon Palace complex opens up some of the largest ceremony and reception spaces in Cancún — beach locations, terraces and ballrooms.

Wedding groups earn as they grow. Ten to nineteen rooms earns a one-hour private cocktail reception; twenty-five rooms earns nine hours of private events; fifty-one and above earns fifteen. Every room in the block needs a minimum three-night stay.

A wedding group with thirty-one paid room nights earns a complimentary room and an upgrade — the first of each reserved for the couple. Wedding credit of $20 per paid room night goes toward florals, décor, cake, photography and entertainment, and the whole group gets 15% off the spa and 40% off tee times.

Package pricing is confirmed directly with Palace before it goes in your quote.

Two tiers, and the second one is where it gets good.

From 30 combined room nights: kids and teens seventeen and under stay free, roundtrip airport transfers are included on a four-night minimum, and each room earns $250 in resort credit. You also get a discounted group rate and a reservation for the whole group to dine together.

From 10 rooms at three nights each: everything above, plus one complimentary room and one complimentary upgrade — to the next category, up to the Honeymoon Suite — and free event hours on the terrace or the beach.

Two things to budget for. A 16% service fee applies to anything paid with resort credit, and complimentary rooms still carry $25 per person per night for taxes and gratuities.

Kids and teens. The Playroom kids’ club is included, with food and drinks, for ages four to twelve — under-fours are welcome with an adult. There is a scaled-down supermarket, a handicrafts area and video gaming here, and a much larger Playroom at Moon Palace Cancún with a mini cinema, dollhouse, fashion catwalk and arcade. The Dreamery runs kids’ programming at both.

Group rates are arranged through the Palace call centre rather than online.

What there is to do

Golf. The Moon Palace Golf Club is a 27-hole Jack Nicklaus course, effectively steps from your room, with a second Nicklaus course at Club Riviera Cancún and a golf academy on site.

In the water. Kayaking, paddleboarding, snorkelling and catamaran sailing are included — at least one adult is required to operate the equipment.

O Spa. Thirty-five treatment rooms, a hydrotherapy circuit and a Golden Suite. O Princess offers kid-friendly spa treatments for girls aged four to twelve, at additional cost.

Evenings. Nightly shows and live music, with headline concerts at Moon Palace. Nizuc guests get complimentary access to Unique Nightclub with a disco pass from either lobby.
