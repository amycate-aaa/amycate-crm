The Property

Big, new, and built for families

Riu Palace Aquarelle sits on White Bay Beach in Falmouth, the historic Georgian port town roughly midway between Montego Bay and Ocho Rios. It is a large, recently built all-inclusive — 753 rooms across a sprawling site — and it leans hard into the family market: four outdoor pools, a separate children’s pool, the Splash Water World water park with slides, and the RiuLand kids’ club.

The Riu formula is consistent and easy to explain to guests: everything included, 24 hours a day, no reservation system for most restaurants, liquor dispensers and a restocked mini-bar in every room. Seven restaurants and five bars, with reservations needed only at the Steakhouse, Kyoto and Napoli. Service runs around the clock, which matters more than it sounds for a group arriving on a late flight.

What makes it easy to sell is the beach and the price point. What makes it a narrower fit than it first appears is the wedding programme — there is exactly one venue, and it caps at 100. Read the planning notes before you promise anything larger.

Guests also hold exchange privileges with Riu Montego Bay next door, which is adults-only 18+. That gives the adults in a group somewhere quieter to go for a day without changing hotels.

Weddings

One venue — the beach

This is the whole wedding programme. There is no garden gazebo, no terrace, no ballroom option published. Everything happens on White Bay Beach, and it caps at 100.

Beach

Up to 100 guests
Ceremony · Cocktail Hour · Reception

Whether the couple wants an intimate sunset ceremony or a full daytime affair, White Bay Beach carries all three parts of the day — vows, cocktails and dinner — in one setting. Toes in the sand, ocean directly behind, and a choice of décor treatments from classic to modern.

Because it is the only venue, the weather plan and the guest count are the two conversations to have first.

Planning

What to raise on the call

Selling points

  • Genuinely strong family inventory — two-bedroom layouts sleeping four to six, and one Superior with two bathrooms
  • Splash Water World and RiuLand make it an easy sell to guests bringing kids
  • All-inclusive runs 24 hours, and most restaurants take walk-ins — simple to explain to a large guest list
  • Brand-new build, so rooms and public areas show well in photos
  • Exchange privileges with adults-only Riu Montego Bay give the grown-ups a quieter day out
  • Falmouth is a real town — Georgian architecture, markets, rum tastings — for guests who want off-property
  • Swim-up ocean-view junior suite is a strong, affordable upgrade for the couple

Watch-outs

  • One venue, 100 guests. A larger group has no published alternative here — qualify the headcount before anything else
  • No indoor or covered venue is published, so the rain plan is an open question worth asking about early
  • Both swim-up categories are 18+ — do not put a family with children in one
  • Ocean Front and Suite categories sleep 2 only; families need the Junior Suite or Family Room tiers
  • 2 weddings a day, so date and time slot want locking early
  • It is a large resort — guests wanting intimate and quiet may be happier elsewhere
Internal — not for client materials
  • DWT lists this as a Sapphire partner — lower tier than the Platinum properties, so check what group concessions actually apply
  • Location naming is inconsistent across sources: the resort is in Falmouth, Trelawny, but DWT files it under Montego Bay and Destify has a page calling it Cancun. Use Falmouth with clients and set expectations on transfer time from MBJ
  • Star rating conflict: DWT says 4.5, Riu’s own materials and several resellers say 5. Neither is authoritative — avoid leading with a number
  • Room count conflict: DWT highlights say 753, the body copy says “over 700”. Using 753
  • DWT publishes no property-specific venue photograph for this resort — the venues page falls back to generic Riu brand images. The beach shot on this page is the resort aerial, which does show White Bay. Do not lift the DWT venue image into a client quote thinking it is this property
  • Riu’s photo set includes a “2 bedroom swim-up family room” that does not appear in DWT’s room list — possibly an unpublished category. Worth asking about for a family that wants swim-up but has children
  • No square footage published for the Suite, 2 Bedroom and Family Room categories — only the 377 sq ft junior suites are documented
  • Wedding Collection pricing is not published by DWT. Confirm current pricing and the free-wedding qualifying terms before quoting
