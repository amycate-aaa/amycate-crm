The resort

The biggest wedding canvas in Jamaica — on a property that has been open barely a year

Every suite is ocean-view. Every suite is at least 560 sq ft. And a 1,088-guest private island sits offshore.

Princess Grand Jamaica opened in December 2024 on a peninsula in Green Island, Hanover, roughly midway between Montego Bay and Negril. It is part of a US$400 million, two-resort campus — the family-friendly Grand alongside the adults-only Princess Senses The Mangrove — built across more than 100 acres of mangrove and beachfront.

The scale is the story. 590 suites, none smaller than 560 sq ft, all ocean-view, all with a balcony or terrace. Nine restaurants, nine bars, three pools and an aquatic park with slides. And Dream Island — a private island offshore that seats 1,088 — alongside a 1,185-guest ballroom complex. Very little in the Caribbean can host a wedding this large without going off-property.

Two things to raise before you quote a date. The resort closed after Hurricane Melissa in late October 2025 and reopened on 1 March 2026; Princess Senses reopened a month earlier. The brand has not published a venue-by-venue operational list since, so confirm the specific ceremony and reception spaces are back in service for the couple’s date.

And the casino is not open. Jamaica issued its first casino licence for this property in July 2026, with the operator targeting the last quarter of 2026 and a regulatory deadline of March 2027. It is a real draw once it lands — but it cannot be sold as available today.

Planning notes

Requirements, timing and what to confirm

A newer property with a hurricane closure behind it — more than usual needs checking against the couple’s actual date.

Booking & legal

  • Book at least six months in advance
  • Non-refundable deposit of $300 to hold a date
  • Couple must arrive at least 48 business hours before the ceremony
  • Ceremony runs 20–30 minutes
  • Judge, minister, Pandit and legal application fees may apply on top of the package

Confirm venue-by-venue availability. The resort closed after Hurricane Melissa in October 2025 and reopened 1 March 2026. Princess has not published a facility-level operational list since, so check the specific ceremony and reception spaces for the couple’s date rather than assuming the full inventory is back.

The casino is not open. Jamaica’s first casino licence was issued for this property in July 2026; the operator is targeting the last quarter of 2026, with a regulatory deadline of March 2027. Do not sell it as an amenity yet.

Cross-resort access is not symmetric. Guests of the adults-only Princess Senses The Mangrove next door get full access to both properties; Grand guests get access tailored to age group and room category. If the party is split across both resorts, confirm exactly who can go where before anyone books.

The transfer is about 55 km from Sangster International in Montego Bay — longer than the Montego Bay strip resorts, shorter than most of Negril.
