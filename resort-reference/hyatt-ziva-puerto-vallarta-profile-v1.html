What this resort is

Hyatt Ziva Puerto Vallarta sits on its own private cove on Las Estacas Beach, about thirty minutes from the airport and five from downtown. It is all-ages and all-inclusive, 335 suites across two towers, and it is smaller and more intimate than most resorts of its class.

The property divides cleanly. The Main Tower holds the 473 sq ft rooms — garden, ocean view, oceanfront, and a swim-up row on the ground floor. The Club Tower holds the 704 sq ft suites, every one with a private furnished balcony, plus the lounge, the plunge pool suite on the top floor and the two-storey Presidential penthouse. Almost every room looks at the Pacific.

Dining is stronger than the size suggests: teppanyaki and sushi, a firewood-oven Italian kitchen, an authentic Jalisco hacienda, a seaside grill and an international buffet, plus a rooftop bar over the bay. Four infinity pools, an adults-only pool with its own bar, a children's pool and an oversized infinity hot tub. VitaMar Spa looks straight out to sea.

For a wedding it is unusually flexible. The oceanfront gazebo takes forty, the beach takes two hundred, the ballroom takes six hundred, and the wedding for two is complimentary with a seven-night stay in a Club Ocean View Suite or higher. For a group it is a genuine meetings property, with a divisible ballroom, five breakout rooms and outdoor space for seven hundred standing.

Rooms and suites

Eighteen categories. Every room has a flat-screen TV, individual climate control, a rainfall shower, robes and slippers, a coffee maker, an in-room safe and a minibar restocked daily with beer, soft drinks and snacks. Sockets are standard North American.

Three things worth knowing before you choose

Connecting rooms are not available. Families who want to be next door should be booked as adjacent rooms on the same floor, requested at booking and confirmed on arrival.

Only two rooms sleep five — Club Ocean View Suite Double and Club Oceanfront Hot Tub Double. Nothing sleeps six, and there is no two-bedroom category on the property.

The swim-up rooms have age rules. Main Tower swim-ups are 18 and over. Club Tower swim-up suites are 13 and over. Oceanfront means an unobstructed panoramic view; ocean view may be partial.

Dining and drinks

Seven restaurants and five bars, all included, plus 24-hour in-suite dining. Reservations are recommended for the specialty restaurants and required for the teppanyaki tables at Tamari, booked through the Culinary Concierge. Children's menus are available at every restaurant, and restaurants close at 10:30 or 11:00 in the evening.

The resort publishes three dress codes — Resort Casual, Smart Casual and Resort Formal. Across all of them: shoes, shorts and tops must be worn, nobody barefoot, and no wet clothes in any restaurant. Planning your event

Nine spaces, indoor and outdoor, from a gazebo for forty to a beach for seven hundred standing. Capacities below are what the resort publishes, with seated and standing shown separately so you can size a room honestly.

The complimentary wedding, exactly as it works

The Just for the Two of You package is complimentary if the couple stays seven nights or more in a Club Ocean View Suite King or Double, or higher. It is available Monday to Wednesday between 9:00 AM and 1:00 PM only, and must be requested at least six months before the wedding date.

Intimate Elegance is complimentary on a different test: 25 room nights or more combined across the group, with a minimum of three nights per room, in qualifying categories. Same Monday to Wednesday morning window, same six-month notice.

Outside those windows the packages are simply purchased, and can be held between 9:00 AM and 4:00 PM.

Rooming a group here

Two things shape every group block at this resort. Connecting rooms are not available, so families who expect to be joined through a door need adjacent rooms requested instead. And only two categories sleep five — Club Ocean View Suite Double and Club Oceanfront Hot Tub Double — with nothing sleeping six. If your group has larger families in it, those two categories need holding early, before the block fills with couples.

Group rates, complimentary room ratios and the payment schedule come with the contract rather than from the resort's published material. Tell me your rough room count and I will get you real numbers.
