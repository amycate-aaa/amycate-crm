Resort Notes

Outdoor private events must be over by 10:30 PM. Cocktail hours and private events held on the beach or a terrace carry a set-up fee of $6 per person for a cocktail hour, $12 for a private event, or $18 for both, ages 4 and up. Outside vendors are charged $1,500 per service per day; the fee and vendor passes are waived when the vendor stays on property at least three nights, and only for photographers, videographers, hair and make-up artists, and DJs without equipment. Local Dominican Republic vendors are not permitted on property. At least 80% of wedding guests must stay at the resort, day passes are capped at 20% of those attending, and day-pass guests must leave by 11:00 PM. The couple arrives two business days before a symbolic ceremony and three before a Catholic ceremony. There is no minimum-night stay requirement. Eden Pool is adults-only and allows optional European-style bathing; Isla Pool and Sun Pool are reserved for higher Unity by Hard Rock membership tiers. Casino drinks, golf, spa treatments and bowling lanes sit outside the all-inclusive. As of August 2026 the Family Pool and the Lazy River are listed as closed for maintenance.
