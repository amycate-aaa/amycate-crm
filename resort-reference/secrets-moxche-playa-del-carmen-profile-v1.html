The Property

An adults-only hideaway inside a gated golf community

Secrets Moxché sits on a stretch of white-sand Riviera Maya beach at the northern entrance to Playa del Carmen, inside Corasol — a gated residential community wrapped around the Gran Coyote golf course. It is roughly forty minutes from Cancún International and about ten minutes from Quinta Avenida, which is the sweet spot: guests get quiet and space, but Fifth Avenue is close enough for an afternoon.

The design is what sets it apart. Rather than the usual pool-and-palapa layout, the resort is built around water features drawn from the Yucatán: seven pools in total, including three man-made cenotes and an interior beach-inspired pool ringed with white sand and palms. The cenotes are full swimming environments, not decoration, and they photograph unlike anything else on this coast.

All 485 suites are junior-suite size or larger, in a contemporary, earthy palette. Everything runs on Unlimited-Luxury® — all dining, premium drinks, Wi-Fi, room service and entertainment included, with no reservations required at most restaurants. The Preferred Club adds a private lounge and a rooftop infinity pool with its own restaurant, the Observatory.

Next door and connected is Impression Moxché by Secrets, a smaller ultra-luxury property with butler service and its own restaurants. Secrets guests can dine at some of it for a fee; Impression guests have full run of both.

Planning

What to raise on the call

Selling points

  • Cenote ceremony photographs are genuinely unique on this coast — nothing else in the Riviera Maya looks like them
  • Rooftop gazebo holds 150 and sits higher than any other ceremony site in Playa del Carmen
  • Sky Wedding Salon is the indoor backup with an ocean view, so a rain plan does not mean a ballroom
  • Ballroom to 300 makes this one of the few adults-only properties that can take a very large or multi-event celebration
  • Preferred Club rooftop is a real upgrade, not a badge — private pool, private elevator, its own restaurant
  • Junior Suites in the two-double layout sleep three, which solves the odd-numbered guest problem
  • Ten minutes to Fifth Avenue keeps guests happy without putting the resort in the middle of town

Watch-outs

  • Strictly 18+ — no exceptions, so it is out for any group with a minor in the party
  • Cenotes Area caps at 40; a guest list that creeps past that loses the signature setting
  • Beyond Memorable has a floor of 50 guests, so a 30-guest group cannot buy it at any price
  • Bisou holds a formal dress code — worth flagging to guests before they pack
  • Adjoining Impression Moxché is a separate property at 16+ with its own rates; do not quote the two interchangeably
  • 2–3 weddings a day at peak, so venue and time slot want locking early
Internal — not for client materials
  • Symbolic and civil/legal ceremonies both available; on-site coordinator included in every package
  • Outdoor music curfew reported at 10:30 p.m. across venues — confirm the current rule with the coordinator before promising a late reception
  • Package cancellation fees run roughly $250 to $750 depending on the package tier
  • Minimum thirty days advance booking; Beyond Memorable roughly one year
  • Preferred Club also unlocks paid add-on dining: omakase sushi, taco and tequila tasting, and Author’s Signature at Impression — these carry a fee and should not be described as included
  • DWT lists Seasoul as Preferred Club exclusive; the resort’s own materials do not. Treat Seasoul as open to all guests
  • DWT quotes larger square footages on several categories than Hyatt does. Figures on this page follow Hyatt
  • Room-night comps and free-wedding thresholds shift with promotions — pull current terms per group rather than reusing a prior quote
