The resort

A Mediterranean village on a Caribbean beach

Ochre facades, arched walkways and lantern-lit plazas — it reads more like a seaside town than a megaresort.

Dreams Dominicus sits directly on Bayahibe Beach, a sheltered crescent of powder sand on the south-eastern coast, twenty minutes from La Romana International Airport.

The Italian-inspired architecture — ochre walls, arched walkways, a maze of garden paths and hidden nooks — gives it the feel of a Mediterranean village rather than a conventional Caribbean resort. Its 488 rooms run an unusually deep ladder, from a 538 sq ft Deluxe to a 2,744 sq ft Presidential, with swim-up categories, a honeymoon suite with a private hot tub and hammock, and connecting family suites that sleep up to eleven.

Nearly twenty restaurants and bars run reservation-free, alongside four pools, an 18,000 sq ft spa, and supervised programmes for both children and teens.

It takes a maximum of two weddings a day — a real point of difference against the high-volume Punta Cana properties, and worth saying out loud to a couple who worry about sharing the beach.

Planning notes

  • Ceremony types: civil/legal, symbolic, multicultural and same-sex
  • Wedding in Paradise is complimentary with 7 nights in a Preferred Club Suite Swim-Up or higher, or 5 rooms in any category for 3 nights — an easy threshold for most groups
  • Maximum two weddings a day, so the property never feels like a conveyor belt
  • Beyond Memorable needs 79 guests and 75 cumulative paid room nights, booked a year ahead
  • All weddings must be booked at least 30 days in advance
  • La Romana airport (LRM) is 20 minutes; Punta Cana (PUJ) is 1.5–2 hours

The Pier is the trap. It seats 120 for a ceremony but only 50 for a reception. A couple who falls for the pier photograph with 80 guests can marry there but must move the dinner. Raise it early.

Check the airport routing before selling this one. It is twenty minutes from LRM but up to two hours from PUJ, and LRM has far thinner US service — that difference can reshape a guest's travel day.
