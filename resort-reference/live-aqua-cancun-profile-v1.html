The resort

An adults-only landmark in the middle of the Hotel Zone

371 rooms on a Blue Flag beach, eighteen and over throughout.

Live Aqua sits at Km 12.5 on Boulevard Kukulcán, in the heart of Cancún’s Hotel Zone. It is adults only — genuinely so, not a tower or a section — which makes it a different proposition from almost everything around it.

The resort leans into sensory design: aromatherapy in the corridors, a pillow menu, chromotherapy jacuzzis in the suites, and a spa built around a water circuit rather than a treatment list. It has been recognised with Blue Flag certification for the beach and sits consistently in Tripadvisor’s top ten for the region.

Nineteen room categories, nine of which carry Aqua Club access. Every suite on the property is a club room.

Pools, beach & water

Four pools and a Blue Flag beach

Pools. The Main Pool, the Aqua Pool, the Azur Pool and the Pool Club, which has its own bar open through the day and becomes a private space at night. Every one is adults-only by definition. Pools open 8am to 8pm, and one of them is heated.

The beach. Blue Flag certified, with complimentary loungers and Balinese beds. Bali beds are included with suite categories; otherwise they can be taken for the day, which includes a twenty-minute foot massage.

The spa water circuit is separate from the pools and worth knowing about: twelve indoor treatment areas and four outdoor, steam baths, saunas and a jacuzzi. Suite guests get unlimited access.

I will confirm current sea conditions for your dates before you travel — that is worth asking about anywhere on this coast, and it is not something the resort publishes.

Weddings & groups

One wedding a day, so the team is yours from beginning to end.

Ceremonies here are symbolic, performed by a non-denominational minister. The resort holds Equality Institute certification for inclusive weddings and works in multicultural traditions.

In every package

A dedicated on-site wedding specialist, the ceremony itself, an altar or gazebo dressed with draping and floral accents, avant-garde white chairs and a ceremony table, bouquet and boutonniere, sound system, cake and a sparkling wine toast.

For the two of you: a complimentary room for one of you on the wedding day, refreshments while you get ready, a room upgrade and late check-out subject to availability, romantic turndown that night, breakfast in bed the morning after, 15% off the spa, and a two-night certificate for your first anniversary.

A convention centre for up to a thousand, and a resort that stays adult all week.

For a milestone birthday, a reunion or an anniversary trip, the appeal here is simple: nobody’s children are running through your dinner, and the whole property is pitched at the same kind of evening.

The spaces. The Grand Aqua Ballroom takes 800 standing or 600 seated and divides down; the beach seats 600; the Azur Pool takes 250 standing. Smaller groups do well on the Presidential Suite Terrace or at the Pool Club, which becomes private at night.

Day passes are worth knowing about if some of your party stays elsewhere. A day pass runs 9am to 6pm, a night pass 6pm to 11pm, and a full day pass covers both and includes an hour of hospitality room. All include restaurants, bars, pools, beach, loungers, towels and the gym.

One thing to plan around. Day passes are not sold when the hotel is more than 95% full, or over holidays. A large group that fills the resort can accidentally lock out its own day guests — so if part of your party is staying off-site, tell me early and I will check it.

Getting there. Airport transfers can be arranged one-way or return, and a four-night stay booked direct includes complimentary transport to the airport. Early check-in and late check-out are both available.

What there is to do

Feel Harmony Spa, a Natura Bissé Prestige Partner. Twelve indoor treatment areas and four outdoors, steam baths, saunas, a jacuzzi, two boutiques and a salon.

Fitness. A gym with Life Fitness and Kinesis equipment, open 6am to 9pm, plus yoga and aquaerobics in the main pool.

Tennis. One court, 7am to 9pm, complimentary for guests.

Evenings. Nightly entertainment, Egos Bar until 1am Monday to Saturday, and the Aka Bar through the day.

Practical. 24-hour room service, doctor on call, boutique and convenience store, valet and self parking with an EV charger, multilingual staff. The resort is smoke-free, and dog friendly at extra cost.
