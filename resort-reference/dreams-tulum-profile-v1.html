Overview

Dreams Tulum Resort & Spa

Colonial architecture, jungle, and a great deal of beach

Set on 44 acres of jungle and sugar-white sand near the Mayan ruins, Dreams Tulum is a AAA Four Diamond family-friendly resort where Mexican colonial architecture meets easy Caribbean luxury. Nearly two miles of beachfront frame 432 rooms and suites, including swim-outs that open onto a heated pool and an adults-only garden section for couples who want quiet. Its real strength is wedding flexibility: twelve venues across three beaches, a Spanish colonial chapel, a secret garden gazebo and an oceanfront rooftop. Very few properties in the region give you that much choice.

Resort Notes

The resort has a secluded adults-only section, where the Deluxe Garden View Adults-Only Section rooms sit. Bordeaux is an adults-only dining room, though its terrace is open to families. Bordeaux and Portofino have a formal dress code, and World Cafe asks for casual elegance at dinner. Himitsu's two teppanyaki tables are by reservation, up to eight people per table. Explorer's Club is for ages 3-12 and children must be fully potty-trained. The Preferred Club lounge and the private Preferred Club beach area are reserved for Preferred Club guests. Small dogs up to 22 lb are welcome for a nightly fee; they are not permitted at the pool, on the beach, or in the spa, fitness centre or shops.
