The resort

Royalton CHIC Antigua

Royalton CHIC Antigua is the party-leaning end of the Royalton family — adults only, eighteen and over, 235 rooms and suites on Dickenson Bay, about fifteen minutes from V.C. Bird International. It is loud by design: foam pool parties, a resident DJ on the beachfront infinity pool, weekly themed nights and a pop-up "unexpected happening" somewhere on the property most evenings. What sets it apart from every other CHIC is the water. Pescari is the only overwater restaurant on Antigua, and the resort has three overwater bungalow categories on the sunset side — plunge pools, over-water hammocks and glass floor panels — topped by a two-bedroom Chairman bungalow at just over 3,000 square feet. Diamond Club is the upgrade that changes the stay here rather than decorating it: butler service, a private pool, a reserved beach area with waiter service, and breakfast in a separate restaurant. It suits couples who want a wedding with a nightlife attached, friend groups, milestone birthdays and vow renewals. It is not the resort for anyone travelling with children, and it is not the resort for a quiet week.
