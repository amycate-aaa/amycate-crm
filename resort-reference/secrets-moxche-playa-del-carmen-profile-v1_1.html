The Resort

An adults-only landmark on the Riviera Maya

Secrets Moxché is an all-suite, adults-only resort set on a white-sand stretch of the Riviera Maya, ten minutes from Playa del Carmen's Quinta Avenida. Every one of its 485 suites has a furnished balcony or terrace, and the design leans earthy and contemporary — wood, marble and stone drawn from its Yucatán surroundings.

The scale is what sets it apart. Seven heated pools, three man-made cenotes unique to the property, a large interior beach, and eighteen restaurants and bars including a AAA Four Diamond French room. The Preferred Club adds a rooftop infinity pool, a private lounge and its own rooftop restaurant. For weddings there are eight venues, from a rooftop sky gazebo to a ballroom that seats three hundred, and two to three weddings a day at peak season — which is the reason to lock a date and a time slot early.

Upgrade

The Preferred Club

The Preferred Club is the resort's club tier: an upgraded suite in a premium location, a private lounge, and its own rooftop level with an infinity pool, bar and restaurant. Since the resort is adults-only throughout, the club is simply a quieter, more serviced layer on top of it.

Worth knowing for a wedding party: club status is per suite, so an upgraded couple gets the rooftop and the lounge, but their guests in resort suites will not.

  • Personalized check-in and check-out
  • Access to the Preferred Club lounge and bar, daily 10:00 a.m.–11:00 p.m.
  • Daily continental breakfast service
  • Afternoon hot and cold hors d'oeuvres
  • Exquisite desserts and premium liquors
  • Exclusive access to the rooftop infinity pool, bar and restaurant
  • Preferred Club suites in premium locations

Planning Notes

Things to settle early

Ceremony types

Symbolic and civil/legal ceremonies are both offered. A judge or minister carries a separate fee confirmed by the coordinator.

Booking windows

Every wedding needs a minimum of 30 days' notice. Beyond Memorable is built around roughly a year, though the resort can release it sooner.

Volume at peak

Two to three weddings a day in high season. Venue and time slot should be held as early as the date itself.

Music curfew

Outdoor music is reported to stop at 10:30 p.m. Confirm the current rule with the coordinator before planning a late outdoor reception.

Rooftop capacity

The Sky Rooftop Gazebo takes about 60 seated. The larger 150 figure is a standing cocktail number, not a seated ceremony.

Age policy

Adults-only throughout. The adjoining Impression Moxché by Secrets is a separate property with its own age policy and its own rates.

Occupancy

Almost every category caps at two guests. Only the two-double Junior Suite Tropical View takes a third. There are no connecting or two-bedroom suites published.

Paid extras

Omakase, the taco and tequila tastings, the Author's Signature restaurant at Impression, spa treatments, catamaran, scuba and deep-sea fishing all carry a fee.
