The resort

Big enough for every generation of the guest list

680 rooms in Ocho Rios, and the largest Palace property in Jamaica.

Moon Palace Jamaica is a sprawling all-inclusive on the north coast, built for groups that span three generations. A FlowRider® surf simulator, a lazy river, several pools and a splash park keep the younger half occupied; the spa, the steakhouse and the sunset stretch of beach handle everyone else.

For weddings it is one of the few properties in Jamaica that can genuinely take a large party — South Beach seats up to 900, which is among the biggest outdoor settings on the island, and Starlight Terrace takes 600.

The Palace wedding programme runs from a complimentary tier up to fully custom, including three Mahal collections for South Asian celebrations.

Accommodation

Ten categories, and two that sleep seven

The Family Deluxe and the Presidential are the two-room layouts. Everything else runs from three to five guests, with a rollaway covering the fifth.

Sleeps 3 · French balcony

Deluxe Resort View

The entry category, and still comfortable. A French balcony over the resort grounds, 24-hour room service, minibar, coffee maker, CHI bath products, a smart TV with Chromecast, welcome wine and nightly turndown.

Sleeps 5 · Private balcony

Resort View with Balcony

The same room with a proper private balcony, table and two chairs. A rollaway takes a fifth guest, which makes this the cheapest room here that a family of five fits into.

Sleeps 3 · French balcony

Deluxe Ocean View

Same layout, turned to face the Caribbean. French balcony, 24-hour service, minibar, coffee maker, CHI amenities and a smart TV.

Sleeps 5 · Private balcony

Deluxe Ocean View with Balcony

Ocean-facing with a full balcony and seating, plus a top-shelf in-room liquor dispenser. Rollaway for a fifth guest.

Sleeps 2 · Adults only

Superior Honeymoon Deluxe

For couples, and genuinely so — exclusive access to a private beach area with its own waiter service. Aromatherapy and pillow menus, nightly petit fours, in-room snacks and a premium liquor dispenser.

Sleeps 5 · Terrace & hammock

Superior Walk Out Suite

Ground floor, with a private terrace and hammock stepping straight out onto the grounds. Double whirlpool tub, aromatherapy and pillow menus, in-room snacks.

501 sq ft · Sleeps 5

Superior Junior Suite Ocean View

Noticeably larger at 501 sq ft, with a balcony, a double whirlpool tub and L’Occitane bath amenities. The first category that feels like a suite rather than a room.

Sleeps 5 · Separate living & dining

Superior Governor Suite

A separate living and dining area, double whirlpool tub, and a balcony with table and chairs. Room to have people in rather than just sleep.

646 sq ft · Sleeps 7 · Two connecting rooms

Superior Family Deluxe

Two connecting rooms — a king room joined to a kids’ room with two double beds. Kid-sized robes and slippers, L’Oréal Kids bath products and their own amenities. The reason most families book here.

Sleeps 7 · Two bedrooms

Superior Two Bedrooms Presidential Suite

The top of the property. Two bedrooms, two whirlpool tubs on a private balcony, a separate living room and a dining and bar area, two full bathrooms plus a half bath.

One thing worth knowing. Resort View with Balcony and Deluxe Ocean View with Balcony are photographed with the same image on the resort’s own site — the rooms differ only in which way they face. Ask me and I will describe the difference properly.

Pools, beach & water

A lazy river, a FlowRider and a splash park

The water is the draw here. A FlowRider® double wave simulator, a lazy river winding through the grounds, multiple pools with swim-up bars, and a dedicated splash park for younger children.

The beach runs along the Ocho Rios coast, with paddleboarding and space enough that a 900-guest event fits on it.

I confirm current sea conditions for your dates before you travel — worth asking anywhere on this coast, and not something the resort publishes.

Three Mahal collections are built specifically for South Asian weddings. Pricing on the upper tiers is quoted per date and headcount rather than published.

Room for a very large group, and enough to do that nobody is bored.

The same six spaces work for a milestone birthday, a reunion or a corporate incentive. South Beach at 900 and Starlight Terrace at 600 are the two that make a genuinely large celebration possible; the gazebos and decks suit forty to a hundred and forty.

What keeps a mixed group happy. The FlowRider® and the lazy river handle teenagers, the splash park handles small children, and the spa and steakhouse handle the adults. Room service runs 24 hours, which matters when a group lands late.

Rooms for families. Two categories sleep seven — the Superior Family Deluxe, which is two connecting rooms with its own kids’ amenities, and the Two Bedrooms Presidential. Several categories take a fifth guest on a rollaway.

Group rates, room blocks and comp-room ratios are quoted directly. Send me your dates and rough numbers and I will put it together.

Days

What there is to do

In the water. FlowRider® double wave simulator, lazy river, pools with swim-up bars, splash park, paddleboarding off the beach.

The spa. A full spa on property, with treatment rooms and a hydrotherapy area.

Evenings. International shows and live entertainment, the Piano Bar, and a steakhouse for a dressed-up dinner.

Off property. Ocho Rios puts the river falls, Dunn’s River and the north coast within easy reach. Excursions are booked through the concierge.
