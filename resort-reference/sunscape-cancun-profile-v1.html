The resort

A family resort with a proper teen club

458 rooms and suites on the Caribbean side of the Hotel Zone, smoke-free and all ages.

Sunscape sits in the middle of Cancún’s Hotel Zone, on the Caribbean side, thirty minutes from the airport. It is an all-ages, smoke-free resort with a pirate-themed splash park, seven waterslides and two splash zones.

What sets it apart from most of the family resorts in Cancún is Core Zone — a genuine teen club for thirteen to seventeens with published hours, arcade games, consoles, air hockey and billiards. Younger children have the Explorer’s Club with Red Cross certified staff.

The upgraded tier here is Sun Club, and unusually it is family-usable rather than adults-only.

Pools, beach & water

Pools, a pirate splash park and seven waterslides

Pools. An activity pool, a family pool, two splash pools with slides and a hot tub. Pool waiter service throughout, and certified lifeguards in both pool and beach areas.

The splash park comes complete with a pirate ship and a medieval castle, seven waterslides and two splash zones.

The beach is a private stretch of powdery white sand on the Caribbean, with cabanas, waiter service, oceanfront yoga and live entertainment.

Kids’ menus are available at every restaurant, and no dining venue is adults-only.

Free nights, a welcome amenity and a complimentary upgrade.

Booking a group here earns free nights, a signature welcome drink, a special welcome amenity for one designated room, and one complimentary upgrade to the next room category subject to availability. Group check-in for common arrivals is arranged from fifteen rooms.

Kids and teens. The Explorer’s Club covers ages four to twelve, 9am to 6pm, with a pirate splash park, seven waterslides, two splash zones and a family pool, plus arts and crafts, movie nights and beach games. Staff are certified by the Red Cross. Core Zone covers thirteen to seventeen, 1pm to 9pm, with arcade games, consoles, air hockey and billiards. Both are included in the all-inclusive plan.

Babysitting is available after hours for ages four to twelve at additional cost.

Worth budgeting for. An environmental tax of 79 Mexican pesos per room per night is collected on arrival. There is no resort fee.

Days

What there is to do

Included. Wine and tequila tastings with an on-site sommelier, beach volleyball and soccer, yoga including oceanfront sessions, aqua aerobics, spinning, a guacamole-making class and daily pool parties.

In the water. Non-motorised water sports are part of the all-inclusive plan, and there is an introduction to scuba diving.

Aura Spa. Body rituals, massages, a hydrotherapy circuit with steam bath, outdoor tub and sauna, and a beauty salon — including options designed for teens and children. Spa and salon services are an additional charge.

Evenings. Live music, mariachi bands, theme parties, nightly shows and performances at the lobby bar.
