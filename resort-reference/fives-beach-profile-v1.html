Overview

The Fives Beach Hotel & Residences

Where It All Comes Together — Endless Possibilities, One Destination

The Fives Beach Hotel & Residences is a AAA Four Diamond, residence-style resort on Xcalacoco Beach in Playa del Carmen — about 40 minutes from Cancún International Airport and 10 minutes from Fifth Avenue downtown. Unlike traditional all-inclusives, it is built around 1-, 2- and 3-bedroom residence suites with full kitchens (many with private penthouse rooftop lounges and whirlpools), making it a standout for multi-generational families and wedding groups who want space. The experience rests on three pillars: All Residence-Style Suites®, À la Carte All-Inclusive® (a curated restaurant collection rather than typical buffet fare), and Joy & Wellness®. On property: 7-8 pools, 15 restaurants and gourmet corners, 6 bars, a full-service spa built over a natural cenote with a dedicated bridal suite, kids and teens clubs, tennis and basketball courts, a game room, and event facilities. For weddings, it uniquely offers NO TIME RESTRICTIONS on events, consecrated Catholic venues, and covered ocean-view or jungle-view venues with air conditioning.
