Overview

Dreams Los Cabos Suites Golf Resort & Spa

Desert meets the sea at Land's End — Los Cabos' most scenic all-inclusive for destination weddings and honeymoons.

Dreams Los Cabos Suites Golf Resort & Spa is perfectly positioned in one of Mexico's most iconic natural settings, where the Baja Peninsula's dramatic desert landscape meets the turquoise waters at the convergence of the Pacific Ocean and the Sea of Cortez. Four wedding venues leverage this extraordinary backdrop, with sweeping ocean views, legendary Baja sunset skies, and the famous Arch of Cabo San Lucas visible in the distance. Seven wedding packages within an Unlimited-Luxury® framework give couples the tools to build their perfect celebration while their guests enjoy world-class amenities and activities. Los Cabos' world-class airport connections, year-round sunshine, and unique combination of desert adventure and beach luxury make it one of the most versatile and sought-after destinations for wedding celebrations in all of Mexico.

    Resort Notes

    The Tranquility pool is reserved for guests 18 and over. Bordeaux is adults-only and the only restaurant with a formal dress code; the others range from casual to casual elegance. The Explorer’s Club is for ages 3–12 and children must be fully potty-trained. Golf is played at nearby championship courses at preferred rates rather than on site. Music at outdoor events finishes at 10:30pm.
