Overview

Hyatt Ziva Cancun

Vibrant Escape in the Heart of Cancun

Hyatt Ziva Cancun is a stunning all-inclusive resort featuring 547 luxury suites on a prime beachfront location in the Cancun Hotel Zone. All suites feature designer accents, spa-like bathrooms with rainfall showers, furnished balconies, and sweeping Caribbean Sea views. The resort offers two distinct experiences: a family-friendly main resort and the exclusive adults-only Turquoize Tower (18+), with private hot tubs on every balcony, a rooftop infinity pool, and direct access to Zen Spa. With 8 specialty restaurants, 11 bars and lounges, swim-up suites, Club Level access, and one of the most diverse room inventories in Cancun, this is an ideal property for FIT clients and groups alike.

Resort Notes

A family resort with an adults-only tower, so room choice matters: the six Turquoize categories are strictly 18 and over and cannot be booked by anyone travelling with children, and the Swim-Up King and Swim-Up Double carry a minimum age of 13. Fitness equipment is for guests 18 and over. Minimum age for check-in is 18. Smoking, including electronic cigarettes, is prohibited in suites, on suite decks and balconies, and throughout most common areas, dining venues and bars; designated smoking areas are provided, and a cleaning fee may be charged for smoking in a smoke-free area. Pets are not allowed. The resort accepts Visa, Mastercard and American Express only, and a pre-authorisation hold is placed on the card at check-in. Pre-arrival check-in is not offered. Tap water is not for drinking; filtered and bottled water is provided throughout, and the water and ice served at all resort venues are filtered.
