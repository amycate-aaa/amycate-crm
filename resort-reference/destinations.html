Where To?

The places I send people, and how to choose between them.

Most people come to me knowing the feeling they want — a beach, a week with everyone in one place, a wedding that doesn't take a year to plan — and not the country. This is the short version of what I'd tell you on a call: where each place is, how long the flight is, what the beach and weather are like, what it costs relative to the others, and what it's best for. Pick two or three that sound right and I'll price real resorts.
